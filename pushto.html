Lower sensitivity picks up more (fainter, noisier) points; raise it if the view is cluttered with false detections. If nothing is detected at all, raise Brightness boost first — sensitivity can't find stars the camera isn't capturing.

Quick test: point at the Moon or brightest star you can find first — if that doesn't show up even near max boost, the camera itself isn't gathering enough light through the eyepiece (try a lower-power/wider eyepiece, which passes more light).
